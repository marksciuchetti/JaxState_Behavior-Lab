Reading list for the workshop on remote, online consumer research with iMotions Online: webcam eye tracking, webcam facial expression analysis of video advertisements, online surveys and participant panels, data quality checks such as attention checks and attrition, and informed consent and privacy when participants record themselves at home. Every new entry below was checked on 2026-10-10 against the publisher, repository, library, or index pages that its verification line names, and the bibliographic details are recorded as those pages returned them; fields that no page showed are left blank rather than guessed. Entries reused from the Workshop 2, 3, and 5 lists keep their earlier verification lines, which end with a note naming the list they come from. Entries are in APA 7 style and sorted alphabetically within each group.

The candidate list named Holmqvist and colleagues (2023) as the eye tracking reporting guideline, but that article has been retracted; the 2023 edition of the minimal reporting guideline by Dunn and colleagues (2024) takes its place. Students who see the 2023 article cited elsewhere should treat it as withdrawn.

Core list: 35 sources. Counts by group: Webcam eye tracking = 9; Remote facial coding of advertising = 4; Online samples and panels = 5; Data quality, attention, and attrition = 4; Online experiment standards, timing, and devices = 3; Ethics, consent, and privacy = 3; Statistics and reporting = 7.
