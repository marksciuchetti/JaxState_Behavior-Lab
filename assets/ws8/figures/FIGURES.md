# Workshop 8 figure set

Figures extracted from iMotions PDFs held in the lab folder C:\JaxState_Behavior Lab. iMotions A/S has given Dr. Sciuchetti permission to share these materials with his class. Every figure is a PNG no wider or taller than 1600 px. Workshop 8 covers analyzing and reporting results with iMotions Lab 11.1: opening an analysis and the Aggregate replay editor (panels, aggregated signals, stimulus annotations), heatmaps (how they are computed, settings, gaze heatmap versus spotlight and beeswarm, what a heatmap cannot show, export), scan paths, the Emotional Heatmap, AOI metrics, the Raw Data Aggregation and Epoching notebooks and their outputs, the HTML Reports, the single multimodal export and a do it yourself pivot table and circumplex chart, and the iMotions standard methodology for TV and digital ad testing. Fourteen figures come from four vendor sample client reports (Food Testing, In-Store Shopper, Website UX for Website UX Sample Report, and the Soft Drink Report) and show the structure and visuals of a client report: title and contents, background and objectives, modalities and study details, KPI tables, charts comparing versions, heatmap pages, interpretation and recommendations. The sample reports are vendor deliverables made for teaching and sales; they are not evidence for any claim about advertising or product effects. The worked examples are the vendor studies shown in the manuals (for example Basket_Gorilla, Deconstruction, JSBach_CelloSuiteNo1, DEMO - SonyBravia and an advertising study with Doritos, Toyota and Hyundai stimuli); no lab study appears. Three Workshop 7 figures are reused by path (the statistical test decision chart, the Excel pivot table and chart, and the reverse inference drawing). Suggested sections: start, question, design, example, build, collect, methods, numbers, analysis, results, discussion.
