# Workshop 9 figure set

Figures extracted from iMotions PDFs held in the lab folder C:\JaxState_Behavior Lab. iMotions A/S has given Dr. Sciuchetti permission to share these materials with his class. Every figure is a PNG no wider or taller than 1600 px. Workshop 9 covers study design, consent, and the IRB: the four step study design scheme of the article Designing Your Research Study (business question, experiment, hypotheses, deliverables), research questions, hypotheses and variables, between-subjects, within-subjects and mixed designs, randomization and counterbalancing, test plan designs and the Study Builder tools that implement them (study flow selection, nested randomized and pinned blocks, display order, the test plan CSV and Stimuli folder), the iMotions standard methodologies (video content, TV and digital ad, static image and Web UX templates with their modalities, sample specifications and deliverables), sample size and random assignment, cognitive principles for stimulus design, informed consent and data privacy (the respondent consent FAQ and the iMotions Online privacy policy), data handling, session scripts, staffing and the pilot test, cleaning between participants and the lab room. Many of these iMotions articles contain no images, so most figures are passages of their text, rendered from the vector PDF at 240 dpi and cropped to the passage; the screenshots come from the Study Builder and test plan articles, and four figures are slides of the Cognitive Principles Guide. No figure shows a lab study, a respondent or a photograph of a person; the worked examples are the vendor examples (an energy bill design study with Design 1A to Design 2B, the TV ad and website examples of the articles). Suggested sections: start, question, design, example, build, collect, methods, numbers, analysis, results, discussion.
