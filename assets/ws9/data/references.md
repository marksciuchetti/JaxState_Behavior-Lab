Reading list for the workshop on planning a biometric advertising study before any data are collected: turning a client or research question into a comparison, choosing a between participants or within participants design and counterbalancing condition orders, sampling stimuli so that results generalize beyond the ads tested, planning sample size (including simulation based power for mixed designs in which participants and stimuli are both random factors), preregistering the analysis plan, writing informed consent (including consent to record face video and parental permission and assent for minors), protecting privacy and managing biometric data, preparing the IRB application under the Common Rule, and running sessions and pilots. Every new entry below was checked on 2026-10-11 against the publisher, repository, library, index, or official pages that its verification line names, and the bibliographic details are recorded as those pages returned them; fields that no page showed are left blank rather than guessed. Entries reused from the Workshop 5, 6, 7, and 8 lists keep their earlier verification lines, which end with a note naming the list they come from. The regulations and codes (the Belmont Report, 45 CFR 46, the APA Ethics Code, and the NMSBA Code) are cited from their official pages. Entries are in APA 7 style and sorted alphabetically within each group.

No retraction notice appeared for any entry. One entry carries a published correction that students should read with the article: Thabane and colleagues (2010) has a Correction (2023) that raises the pilot sample size in the worked example from at least 75 to at least 289 patients. Wilkinson and colleagues (2016) has an Addendum (2019) that moves the FAIR principles document to go-fair.org. Ulman, Cakar, and Yildiz (2015) appeared in Science and Engineering Ethics, not in the Journal of Business Ethics as the candidate list had it.

Core list: 40 sources. Counts by group: Experimental design and causal inference = 4; Stimulus sampling and generalization = 4; Sample size and power, including simulation = 6; Preregistration and research planning = 4; Research ethics, regulations, and informed consent = 8; Privacy, biometric data, and data management = 5; Research with minors and online participants = 5; Running sessions and pilots = 4.
