---- STEP 4. The chosen design ----
Rule: the smallest number of ads per brand with power >= 0.80 somewhere in the grid, then the smallest number of participants with
power >= 0.80 that also covers the secondary Holm family (n >= 45, STEP 1).
Ads per brand: best power with 3 per brand = 0.510, with 4 per brand = 0.726; 6 per brand reaches the target -> 6 ads per brand (12 ads)
Participants: 48 (power 0.880, MC SE 0.015, MC 95% interval [0.852, 0.908]; formula 0.866; at the strictest Holm step 0.706)
  for comparison: 72 participants with 6 per brand = 0.938; 48 participants with 3 per brand = 0.474
Type I error of the planned test at this design (C1): 0.052 (MC SE 0.010); naive test accepting singular fits: 0.056
Sensitivity to the ad SD: 0.10 -> 0.976; 0.20 -> 0.880; 0.30 -> 0.640
With a participant by brand slope (SD 0.50): Type I error 0.060, power 0.886
H1 at 48 participants: power 0.975 for OR = 2.00 and 1.000 for OR = 4.34
Attrition: Workshop 7 exclusion rate 2/24 = 0.083 -> 48 / (1 - 0.083) = 52.36 -> 53 sessions: 48 main + 5 reserve; hard cap 60 sessions
Remote comparison (Workshop 6: 142 of 260 kept, 54.6%): 48 analyzable participants would need about 88 people to open the link
Stopping rule: stop when 48 participants meet rules R1 to R2, or after 60 sessions, whichever comes first; an excluded participant is
replaced by the next reserve code, who takes the same Latin square row. Outcome data are not analyzed before the stopping point.
