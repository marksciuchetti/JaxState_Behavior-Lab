---- 9. Why Peaks Per Minute divides by Signal Duration ----
Peaks Per Minute = Peak Count / (Signal Duration / 60000). Signal Duration leaves out gaps longer than 4 s,
so a Bluetooth dropout shortens the time in which a peak could have been detected. Dividing by Duration
