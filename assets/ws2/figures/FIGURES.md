## Notes

- Help article figures are the embedded screenshots, extracted once by PDF object ID (the printed pages reference every image on every page) at native resolution, with the soft mask composited on white. Single screenshots are not rescaled unless the longest side exceeds 1600 px. The page given is the page where the image is visible, confirmed by rendering each page at 150 dpi and template matching the image against it. Two pages were corrected by that check (replay_menu_icons_legend is on page 4 and heatmap_aggregation_examples is on page 4, where it is fully visible).
- Page numbers are PDF page numbers (page 1 of each printed help article is its table of contents), not the printed page labels.
- Composites built from rasters of the same article, placed on a white canvas: calib_eye_finder_widget_states (the annotated widget plus five state tiles, pages 4 to 5), aoi_groups_add_assign_menu (three panels, page 9), export_excel_text_import_wizard_steps (three wizard screens, pages 5 to 6) and reencode_preview_and_convert_menu (thumbnail strip above the context menu). The context menu in the last one is enlarged 2 times so that its text is legible; every other figure is at or below native pixel size.
- Crops: staticimage_objectives_modalities_sample and staticimage_methodology_flow are the upper and lower halves of one slide image on page 2 of Standard Methodologies: Static Image Testing. aoi_draw_tools_popup is cropped to the Draw AOI pop-up. calib_9_point_layout has its thin black frame removed. calib_onescreen_sensor_status_window is cropped to the three status tiles so that the respondent name field at the top of the source screenshot is not shown. Blank margins were trimmed from aurora_missing_setup_warning, export_data_quality_statistics_file, export_survey_data_per_respondent_file and gazesmoothing_noisy_gaze_path.
- Eye tracker model shown in the source screenshots: the Smart Eye Setup Tool screens and the Global Settings dialog in the Aurora article show Smart Eye AI-X as the selected model (the article covers both devices and says to select Smart Eye Aurora or Smart Eye AI-X from the drop-down menu), and the Global Settings dialog in the calibration planning article shows a Tobii X2 server model. Item to verify on the lab machine: the exact wording of the model entry for the Aurora in Global Settings, Eye tracking tab.
- Not used: the Aurora EPU diagram (Aurora article, page 8) because the article says the EPU is provided with the Aurora 120 Hz and 250 Hz models, not the lab's 60 Hz unit; the Exposure Summary example and the sensor data editor options, because the exports topic was capped at five figures; the instruction slide for calibration (Planning article, page 5). The Basic Heatmap article has no Gaussian or opacity explanation, only the grid and paint by numbers account, so none is included. The AOI metric definitions are bullet lists in the article, so the hover definition screenshot stands in for a definitions table.
- Avoided because Workshop 1 already has them: studybuilder_new_study_dialog, studybuilder_name_your_study, studybuilder_respondent_screen, aoi_editor_annotated_timeline, aoi_editor_video_metrics_panel, aoi_metrics_panel_static_image, aoimetrics_window_annotated, aoi_export_settings, heatmap_open_from_analysis_menu, heatmap_settings_and_export, scanpath_settings_panels, scanpath_shelf_overlay, ivt_gaze_analysis_settings and ivt_visual_angle_diagram.
