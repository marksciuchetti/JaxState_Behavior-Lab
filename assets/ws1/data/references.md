# Workshop 1 reading list: mobile eye tracking of point of sale material and shelf attention

Verified reference list for the undergraduate workshop built around the iMotions In-Store Shopper sample study (Sensodyne point of sale material, two shelf layouts, eye tracking glasses plus survey). Every entry below was resolved on 2026-10-07 through the DOI registry (Crossref), the OpenAlex DOI index, or the publisher or host page, and the bibliographic details are recorded as the resolver returned them. Nothing that failed to resolve is listed. Entries are in APA 7 style and sorted alphabetically.

Core list: 40 sources. Counts by topic: Mobile eye tracking in retail and shelf studies = 12; Eye tracking methodology = 7; Consumer neuroscience and biometrics in marketing = 6; Research design and statistics for small samples = 9; Ethics of recording and biometric data = 3; Pupil Labs Neon technical sources = 3.

Topic tags: retail_shelf, et_methodology, consumer_neuroscience, design_statistics, ethics, neon_technical. Workshop sections named in the use notes: question, design, methods, analysis, results, discussion, ethics.
