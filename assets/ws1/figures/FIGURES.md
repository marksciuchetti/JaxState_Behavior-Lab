## Notes

- Report pages were rendered whole at 150 dpi with pdftoppm; the title, section divider and virtual reality pages (1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 20, 22) and the self-report deliverables page (11) were not kept.
- Help article figures are the embedded screenshots, extracted once by PDF object ID (the printed pages reference every image on every page). The page given is the page where the image is visible, found by rendering each page at 50 dpi and template matching the image against it; eight of the lower-scoring matches were checked by eye.
- Page numbers are PDF page numbers (page 1 of each printed help article is its table of contents), not the printed page labels.
- neon_phone_data_folder_path.png was rearranged from a 10617 px wide four-panel strip into a 2 x 2 grid so that each Explorer panel stays legible at 1600 px.
- Record Mobile Eye-Tracking Data with Pupil Labs Mobile App was extracted (17 images) but not used: it documents the older Pupil Mobile app and Pupil Player workflow, not the Neon Companion app the lab uses.
- Each help article PDF carries the footer: Confidential, copyright 2023, redistribution is not permitted without written permission from iMotions. The credit line records that permission.
