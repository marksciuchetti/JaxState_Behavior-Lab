## Observations that may differ from the help center wording

- The new study wizard titles are "Create a new study", "Select glasses type", "Select sensors", and "Name your study"; the final button is "Create study". Escape acts as Back inside the wizard, not Cancel.
- The glasses sensor list in the wizard reads "Neon Glasses" and "Pupil Invisible Glasses" (not "Pupil Labs Neon").
- Neon Glasses are chosen only inside the study (wizard, or the study SENSORS tab). Global Settings > Sensors has no Neon or Pupil Labs section, and Global Settings > Eye tracking covers only the screen-based "Eye Tracking Server" model (currently Mouse Simulator, server unchecked) and calibration settings.
- The study-level "GAZE ANALYSIS" tab is the fixation filter page (I-VT), not an analysis launcher. Analyses live as child nodes under the study in the Library tree, and the analysis window has three tabs: REPLAY, AGGREGATE, AOI.
- The study-level bottom buttons for a Neon study are "Neon Glasses Recording: Import data" and "Video Face Blur: Post Process" in addition to "Exposure summaries: Calculate".
- The respondent recording card reports "200Hz" and a percentage (100%) for the Neon recording.
