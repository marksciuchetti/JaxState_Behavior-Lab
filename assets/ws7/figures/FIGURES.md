# Workshop 7 figure set

Figures extracted from iMotions PDFs held in the lab folder C:\JaxState_Behavior Lab. iMotions A/S has given Dr. Sciuchetti permission to share these materials with his class. Every figure is a PNG no wider or taller than 1600 px. Workshop 7 covers getting data out of iMotions Lab 11.1 and making it analysis ready: the study level and analysis level Export views, the Sensor Data export editor and the structure of its files (#INFO, #METADATA, #DATA), R processed metrics exports (GSR, EEG, AFFDEX, respondent statistics), AOI metrics, survey, annotation, data quality, exposure summary, keyboard and mouse, and media exports, conversion to EDF, respondent selection from a CSV file, opening the files correctly in Excel, pivot tables, and the replay and analysis windows where exports start. The worked examples are the vendor studies shown in the manuals (for example Test2_BMW, Basket_Gorilla, Deconstruction, JSBach_CelloSuiteNo1 and VR_Skydiving_Varjo360_ET_EDA); no lab study appears. Suggested sections: start, question, design, example, build, collect, methods, numbers, analysis, results, discussion.
