Reading list for the workshop on getting data out of iMotions Lab and into an analysis-ready, documented, and shareable form: exporting sensor and survey data, organizing exports as tidy data and well-structured spreadsheets, merging streams by respondent, stimulus, and timestamp, writing a codebook and a cleaning log, handling missing data, outliers, and exclusions under rules fixed in advance through preregistration, keeping a reproducible workflow, and sharing data openly while protecting participant privacy. Every new entry below was checked on 2026-10-11 against the publisher, repository, library, or index pages that its verification line names, and the bibliographic details are recorded as those pages returned them; fields that no page showed are left blank rather than guessed. Entries reused from the Workshop 2, 5, and 6 lists keep their earlier verification lines, which end with a note naming the list they come from. Entries are in APA 7 style and sorted alphabetically within each group.

No retraction notice appeared for any entry. The FAIR Guiding Principles article (Wilkinson and colleagues, 2016) has a 2019 Addendum that moves the living document of the principles to go-fair.org/fair-principles; students who follow the link in the original article should use that address instead.

Core list: 33 sources. Counts by group: Tidy data and spreadsheets = 5; Codebooks, documentation, and data management = 4; Reproducible workflows and synchronized recording = 4; Missing data, outliers, and exclusions = 4; Preregistration and analytic flexibility = 4; Open data, data sharing, and privacy = 7; Statistics and reporting = 5.
