Reading list for the workshop on facial expression analysis of emotional response to video advertising, using Affectiva AFFDEX through a webcam in iMotions. Every new entry below was checked on 2026-10-08 against a publisher, repository, library, or vendor page, as each entry records, and the bibliographic details are recorded as the checking source returned them; entries reused from the Workshop 1 and 2 lists keep their earlier checks.
