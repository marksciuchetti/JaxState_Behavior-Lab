iMotions Workshop Series Martin's Family Clothing Consumer Behavior Research Laboratory, Jacksonville State University

Learn to run a biometric study, one workshop at a time

These workshops walk you through the lab's equipment and the iMotions software in the order the work actually happens: plan the study, set up the sensors, collect the data, prepare it, analyze it, and report it. Nothing here assumes you have used iMotions before. Each workshop is a teaching module: numbered steps that explain why before how, "You should see" checkpoints, quick checks, a self check quiz, practice exercises, recorded narration, a glossary, links to the exact help center article and manual for every procedure, and references to published research. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Workshops

Workshop 1

From Question to Publication with the Neon Glasses: An In-Store Shelf Study

A complete teaching module that follows one marketing question from the client's first sentence to a written results and discussion section: the research question and hypotheses, the study design and sample size, the iMotions In-Store Shopper (Sensodyne) worked example, building the study in iMotions Lab 11.1, the field day with the Pupil Labs Neon, gaze mapping and AOIs, reading the export, testing the hypotheses in R or Excel on a simulated dataset, and writing the results, discussion, and methods in publishable form.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 65 figures, 5 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, an 86 term glossary, 40 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 1 →
Workshop 2

From Question to Publication with the Aurora: Testing Two Television Commercials

A complete teaching module for screen based eye tracking: turning a client's question about two commercials into hypotheses, a within subjects design with counterbalanced flows, the Sony BRAVIA demonstration study in the lab Library as the worked example, building the study in iMotions Lab 11.1, setting up the stimulus screen (and setting it up again when the monitor or Windows settings change), setting up and calibrating the Smart Eye Aurora, dynamic AOIs, heatmaps, and exports, paired tests and a mixed model in R or Excel on a simulated data set, and writing the results and discussion for publication.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 31 quick checks, more than 55 figures, 4 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, an 81 term glossary, 35 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 2 →
Workshop 3

From Question to Publication with AFFDEX: Facial Expressions During a Television Commercial

A complete teaching module for facial expression analysis: turning a client's question about a commercial into hypotheses about what viewers' faces display, the lab's campaign ad studies as the model design (Baseline, commercial, Recovery, questionnaire), webcam and lighting setup, enabling Affectiva AFFDEX in iMotions Lab 11.1, post processing and thresholds, the facial expression exports, testing the hypotheses in R or Excel on a simulated data set, and writing what a facial display can and cannot show.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 30 quick checks, 53 figures, 1 video, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 70 term glossary, 36 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 3 →
Workshop 4

From Question to Publication with the Shimmer3 GSR+: Arousal and Heart Rate During Two Commercials

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 30 quick checks, 60 figures, 2 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 76 term glossary, 38 verified references, real pilot practice data with an R script and an Excel workbook, and an Ask the tutor panel. Workshop 8

From Analysis to the Client Report: Effect Sizes, Figures, and Honest Reporting of a Multimodal Ad Study

A complete teaching module for analysis and reporting: turning research and client questions into a written analysis plan, choosing the test for each question, seeing the data with the iMotions analysis tools (aggregate replay, heatmaps, emotional heatmaps, AOI metrics, notebooks, and Reports), effect sizes with confidence intervals, repeated measures models and corrections for multiple tests, descriptive tables and figures, a sensitivity power analysis for the next study, and writing both the academic results and an honest client report from the same simulated study as Workshop 7.

About 5 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 58 figures, 2 videos, 27 script walkthrough cards, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 112 term glossary, 50 verified references, a model client report, and an Ask the tutor panel. First edition: the Analysis Dashboard section was checked on a vendor demonstration study. Available, first edition Open Workshop 8 →
Workshop 9

From Question to Study Plan: Design, Sample Size, Consent, and the IRB for a Biometric Ad Study

A complete teaching module for planning a study before any data are collected: turning a question into a comparison, choosing between and within subjects designs and counterbalancing, building the design in the iMotions Study Builder, consent, the Institutional Review Board, and recruitment, choosing sensors, stimuli, and measures, planning the sample size by simulation, writing a preregistration style analysis plan, scheduling sessions and staff, and running the pilot, with the follow up to the Workshop 8 study as the worked example.

About 5 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 48 figures, 17 script walkthrough cards, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 101 term glossary, 40 verified references, a model consent form, a planning checklist, and an Ask the tutor panel. Always use the current JSU IRB templates; the model form on this page is for practice only. Available, first edition Open Workshop 9 →
