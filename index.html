iMotions Workshop Series Martin's Family Clothing Consumer Behavior Research Laboratory, Jacksonville State University

Learn to run a biometric study, one workshop at a time

These workshops walk you through the lab's equipment and the iMotions software in the order the work actually happens: plan the study, set up the sensors, collect the data, prepare it, analyze it, and report it. Nothing here assumes you have used iMotions before. Each workshop is a teaching module: numbered steps that explain why before how, "You should see" checkpoints, quick checks, a self check quiz, practice exercises, recorded narration, a glossary, links to the exact help center article and manual for every procedure, and references to published research. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Workshop 1

From Question to Publication with the Neon Glasses: An In-Store Shelf Study

A complete teaching module that follows one marketing question from the client's first sentence to a written results and discussion section: the research question and hypotheses, the study design and sample size, the iMotions In-Store Shopper (Sensodyne) worked example, building the study in iMotions Lab 11.1, the field day with the Pupil Labs Neon, gaze mapping and AOIs, reading the export, testing the hypotheses in R or Excel on a simulated dataset, and writing the results, discussion, and methods in publishable form.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 65 figures, 5 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, an 86 term glossary, 40 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 1 →
Workshop 2

From Question to Publication with the Aurora: Testing Two Television Commercials

A complete teaching module for screen based eye tracking: turning a client's question about two commercials into hypotheses, a within subjects design with counterbalanced flows, the Sony BRAVIA demonstration study in the lab Library as the worked example, building the study in iMotions Lab 11.1, setting up and calibrating the Smart Eye Aurora, dynamic AOIs, heatmaps, and exports, paired tests and a mixed model in R or Excel on a simulated data set, and writing the results and discussion for publication.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, more than 55 figures, 4 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, an 80 term glossary, 35 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 2 →

From Question to Publication with AFFDEX: Facial Expressions During a Television Commercial

A complete teaching module for facial expression analysis: turning a client's question about a commercial into hypotheses about what viewers' faces display, the lab's campaign ad studies as the model design (Baseline, commercial, Recovery, questionnaire), webcam and lighting setup, enabling Affectiva AFFDEX in iMotions Lab 11.1, post processing and thresholds, the facial expression exports, testing the hypotheses in R or Excel on a simulated data set, and writing what a facial display can and cannot show.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 51 figures, 1 video, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 70 term glossary, 36 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 3 →

From Question to Publication with the Shimmer3 GSR+: Arousal and Heart Rate During Two Commercials

A complete teaching module for skin conductance and optical heart rate: turning a question about two commercials into hypotheses about arousal, pairing the two Shimmer3 GSR+ units, electrode and sensor placement, baselines and recovery periods, the signal quality and peak detection notebooks, the GSR and heart rate exports, paired tests and a mixed model in R or Excel on a simulated data set, and writing the results with the limits of arousal measures.

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 44 figures, 2 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, a 76 term glossary, 37 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel. Available Open Workshop 4 →

From Question to Publication with the Enobio 8: Frontal Alpha Asymmetry During Video Commercials

About 4 hours over two sittings. Sixteen steps, 29 quick checks, 53 figures, 3 videos, recorded narration, a 12 question self check, 5 practice exercises, an 80 term glossary, 39 verified references, and an Ask the tutor panel.
